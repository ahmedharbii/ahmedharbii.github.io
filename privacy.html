Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 9, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to my personal portfolio website. This Privacy Policy explains how I handle information when you visit ahmedharbii.github.io.

Information Collection

This website is a static portfolio site hosted on GitHub Pages. I do not collect, store, or process any personal data through this website.

What We Don't Collect:

  • No email addresses
  • No names or personal information
  • No tracking cookies
  • No analytics data
  • No IP addresses

Local Storage

This website stores only one preference locally in your browser:

  • Theme Preference: Stores your dark/light mode choice (localStorage)

This data stays on your device, is never transmitted to any server, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

GitHub Pages

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect visitor information as described in their Privacy Statement.

External Links

This website contains links to external sites (LinkedIn, GitHub, Google Scholar, Twitter). I am not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

CDN Services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and Three.js from a CDN. These services may collect usage data as per their respective privacy policies.

Contact

When you click on the email link on the contact page, it opens your default email client. No data is submitted through this website. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact me at ahmedharbii10@gmail.com

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Clear your browser's local storage at any time
  • Use browser privacy features or ad blockers

Children's Privacy

This website does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Compliance

This privacy policy complies with:

  • GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • General web privacy best practices

Summary: This is a simple portfolio website that respects your privacy. I don't track you, collect your data, or use analytics. The only data stored is your theme preference — kept locally on your device.
